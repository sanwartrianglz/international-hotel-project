Airbnb design system — reference storybook

An interactive viewer for 00_inputs/references/airbnb.design.md. Every color, type style, radius, spacing value and component below is rendered from the tokens in that file. Use the sidebar to browse stories and the viewport buttons to preview mobile / tablet behaviour.

Restraint is the system
One accent

Rausch #ff385c appears in only ~3 of 33 components: primary CTA, search orb, saved heart. Everything else is white + ink.

Modest type

Display sits at 22–28px, weight 500–700. The only loud moment is the 64px rating number.

Soft shapes, one shadow

8px buttons, 14px cards, pill search. A single shadow tier; depth comes from photos.

For our project: this is study material only. Airbnb Cereal VF is licensed, so Inter is used here (the file's recommended substitute). Borrow principles — single accent, restraint, 48px touch targets, one shadow — not Airbnb's identity. Our housekeeper app needs its own international palette, RTL support and glove-sized targets.

Inspiration for future getaways

Photo icons (3D illustrated)live site

Next to its thin line icons, the site uses small 3D-rendered object illustrations, a bit like clay or toy models: a brass globe, a house with a tree, a luggage cart, a hot-air balloon, a concierge bell, a door, a clipboard, a calendar, a shield. They are shipped as transparent PNGs (asset folders search-bar-icons and orthographic-images). The nav versions also play a short .webm animation when selected. The icons below are our own redraws in the same style, using housekeeping objects. They are not Airbnb's artwork.

Two styles

A · Perspective (product nav) — 48px

Three-quarter view with a soft ground shadow and rich materials (metal, wood, fabric). Sits beside a text label in the top nav. Plays an animation once when selected.

B · Orthographic (info rows) — 32px

Straight-on view with flatter shading and no ground shadow, so it reads clearly at 32px. Leads a "Things to know" card above the title.

Live behaviour — click a tab

Live behaviour: the selected tab swaps its static PNG for a ~1s webm loop (the house "settles", the balloon bobs, the bell rings), with a 3px ink underline under the tab. Simulated here with a CSS spring.

When to useTop-level categories, announcements, empty states, short policy rows

Photo icon vs line icon

Line (amenities, actions)

Photo (categories, onboarding)

Why this matters for our housekeeper app: object illustrations can be recognised without reading, which supports our icon-first and low-literacy rule. A 3D bucket, towel or bed is understood across languages better than a thin outline. We should commission or build our own set in our own palette and must not reuse Airbnb's PNGs.

Dark, grey & gradient buttonslive site

The site splits button use more finely than the file describes. Rausch is for booking actions, dark is for confirming in a dialog, and grey is for secondary content actions.

Translation patternlive site

The most relevant find for our international project. Machine-translated content is marked with a small translate glyph next to the title, and a grey banner lets the user switch back to the original. The first time it appears, a modal explains the symbol once.

Boutique apartment by the river
Some info has been automatically translated.Show original

Stay in an iconic building, opposite the market and just a few steps from the river.

Direct takeaway for the housekeeper app: notes written by supervisors or guests in one language can be auto-translated into each housekeeper's language. Mark them with the glyph and offer "Show original" so nothing is lost in translation. This supports our multi-language rule without designers making a screen per language.

Skeleton loadinglive site

Before content arrives, every block is drawn as a #ebebeb shape matching the real layout: nav pills, search segments, a section title, and cards with two text lines. The search orb is the only element that renders in full straight away.

Design idea for us: for weak signal on the floor, show a skeleton of the room list straight away from the last offline copy, then refresh it, instead of a blank spinner.
