Restraint is the system
One accent

Rausch #ff385c appears in only ~3 of 33 components: primary CTA, search orb, saved heart. Everything else is white + ink.

Modest type

Display sits at 22–28px, weight 500–700. The only loud moment is the 64px rating number.

Soft shapes, one shadow

8px buttons, 14px cards, pill search. A single shadow tier; depth comes from photos.

For our project: this is study material only. Airbnb Cereal VF is licensed, so Inter is used here (the file's recommended substitute). Borrow principles — single accent, restraint, 48px touch targets, one shadow — not Airbnb's identity. Our housekeeper app needs its own international palette, RTL support and glove-sized targets.
