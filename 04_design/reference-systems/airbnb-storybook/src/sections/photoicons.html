Photo icons (3D illustrated)live site

Next to its thin line icons, the site uses small 3D-rendered object illustrations, a bit like clay or toy models: a brass globe, a house with a tree, a luggage cart, a hot-air balloon, a concierge bell, a door, a clipboard, a calendar, a shield. They are shipped as transparent PNGs (asset folders search-bar-icons and orthographic-images). The nav versions also play a short .webm animation when selected. The icons below are our own redraws in the same style, using housekeeping objects. They are not Airbnb's artwork.

Two styles

A · Perspective (product nav) — 48px

Three-quarter view with a soft ground shadow and rich materials (metal, wood, fabric). Sits beside a text label in the top nav. Plays an animation once when selected.

B · Orthographic (info rows) — 32px

Straight-on view with flatter shading and no ground shadow, so it reads clearly at 32px. Leads a "Things to know" card above the title.

Live behaviour — click a tab

Live behaviour: the selected tab swaps its static PNG for a ~1s webm loop (the house "settles", the balloon bobs, the bell rings), with a 3px ink underline under the tab. Simulated here with a CSS spring.

Style spec

PropertyRule
RenderingSoft 3D look (clay or toy-like): rounded edges, low detail, no outlines or strokes
LightingOne soft key light from the top-left, a gentle rim highlight on edges, shading underneath
MaterialsReal-world materials made a little cuter: brass, painted metal, wood grain hinted by a gradient, matte fabric
ColorWarm and slightly muted. Rausch pink shows up only as an accent (door, shield, tag), never the whole palette
ContainerNone. Transparent background, no tile, no circle
GroundPerspective set: a soft elliptical contact shadow. Orthographic set: no shadow
Sizes48px in nav, 32px in info rows, 64–120px in modals and empty states (e.g. the "one price" tag)
ExportPNG @2x/@3x plus an optional webm for the selected state
When to useTop-level categories, announcements, empty states, short policy rows
When not to useDense controls such as filters, amenities or toolbars. Those keep the 24px, 1.6px-stroke line icons

Photo icon vs line icon

Line (amenities, actions)

Photo (categories, onboarding)

Why this matters for our housekeeper app: object illustrations can be recognised without reading, which supports our icon-first and low-literacy rule. A 3D bucket, towel or bed is understood across languages better than a thin outline. We should commission or build our own set in our own palette and must not reuse Airbnb's PNGs.
