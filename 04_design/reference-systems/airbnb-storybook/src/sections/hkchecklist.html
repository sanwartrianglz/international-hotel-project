Housekeeper room checklistour concept · built from Airbnb patterns

Airbnb has no checklist you tick as you work, so this is our design, assembled from the patterns above: 3D photo icons (Foundations), icon-tile rows (House rules), a photo per step (Arrival guide), the progress count (3 / 6) and a dark "done" button (Actions). Tap a step to tick it, long-press or tap 📷 for the reference photo, and switch the language to see that only the free-text note changes.
